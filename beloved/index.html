10 Our story

A vanity table, not a launch.

Beloved started on a small walnut vanity in a rented flat in Bristol, where Elena Marsh mixed a serum for her own skin because nothing she bought felt kind enough to use every day. Friends asked for jars. Then their sisters did. For three years there was no name on the label, only a date written in pencil.

We still make things that way. A formula stays in testing until the people wearing it stop noticing it and start missing it when it runs out. Every shade is worn for a full season before it gets a name, and every container is built to be refilled, because something you love should not end up in a bin.
