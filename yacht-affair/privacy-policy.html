01Who We Are and Scope

Yacht Affair ("Yacht Affair," "we," "us") is an online marketplace where brokers, dealers, and private sellers advertise yachts for sale, where charter companies advertise yachts for charter, and where buyers and prospective charterers search for them. We are based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, and operate through Bogati Continental Inc.

This policy applies to yachtaffair.com, our other websites and mobile apps, and any service that links to it (together, the "Service"). It forms part of our Terms of Use. We are responsible for the personal information described here: the "controller" under the EU and UK GDPR and the "business" under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial privacy laws, including Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act. Where they apply to you, we also follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), the UK GDPR, the CCPA, and other US state privacy laws.

This policy does not cover information about job applicants or employees, or information you give directly to a third party, such as a broker or charter company whose website you visit from our Service (see Section 7). You are not required to give us personal information, but some features, such as creating an account or posting a listing, will not work without it.

02Information We Collect

We collect information in three ways: directly from you, automatically when you use the Service, and from third parties.

Information you give us.

  • Account and contact details: name, email address, phone number, company, role (broker, dealer, seller, charter operator, or buyer), and login credentials.
  • Listings and advertising content: yacht details, photos, descriptions, prices, locations, licenses, and any contact details you choose to include. Listings are public.
  • Inquiries and messages: the content of messages you send through the Service, including inquiries to a broker, seller, or charter company.
  • Billing information: your name, billing address, and the details we need to charge listing and advertising fees.
  • Communications with us: support requests, feedback, survey responses, and anything else you send us.
  • Public posts: reviews or comments you post in public areas of the Service.

Payment information. Fees you pay to us are processed by our third-party payment gateway, STRIPE, LLC. The gateway collects your card or other payment details. We receive limited information, such as the last four digits of your card, your billing country, and whether a payment succeeded. We do not receive or store your full card number. We do not process or hold payments between buyers, sellers, brokers, or charter companies.

Information collected automatically. When you use the Service, we and our service providers collect your IP address and approximate location, device type, operating system, browser type, pages viewed, links clicked, dates and times of visits, referring pages, and similar usage data, using cookies and similar technologies (see Section 5). On our mobile apps, we collect precise location only if you turn on location services and give permission.

Information from third parties. We may receive information from payment gateways, fraud-prevention services, analytics and advertising providers, and social login services if you use one. We may also collect business contact details from publicly available sources, such as a broker's own website, to invite them to advertise.

What we do not want. Please do not post or send us government ID numbers, Social Insurance or Social Security numbers, bank account numbers, passwords, or other sensitive information.

03How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide the Service: create and manage accounts, publish and display listings, and pass your inquiries to the broker, seller, or charter company you contact.
  • Process listing and advertising fees, send invoices and receipts, and manage subscriptions.
  • Send service messages, such as confirmations, renewal notices, security alerts, and policy updates.
  • Respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • Send marketing emails about Yacht Affair and its advertisers, where you have agreed or the law allows. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Monitor, analyze, and improve the Service, and develop new features.
  • Personalize content and show relevant advertising (see Section 5).
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, scams, spam, security incidents, and other unlawful or prohibited activity (see Section 6).
  • Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our Terms of Use.

Consent for Canadian users. We collect, use, and disclose personal information with your knowledge and consent, which may be express or implied depending on the circumstances. We send commercial electronic messages only in line with Canada's anti-spam law (CASL), and every marketing message includes an unsubscribe link. Even if you unsubscribe from marketing, we may still send transactional or administrative messages.

Legal bases for EEA and UK users. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide the Service you ask for, such as running your account and listing.
  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, and improve the Service, prevent fraud, and send business-to-business communications, where those interests are not outweighed by your rights.
  • Consent: for optional cookies and marketing where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep tax and accounting records and respond to lawful requests.

04When We Share Your Information

We share personal information only in these situations:

  • With the listing party you contact. When you send an inquiry or contact request about a yacht or charter, we pass your name, contact details, and message to the broker, seller, or charter company concerned. After that, they use your information under their own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Public listings. Listings and any contact details you include in them are visible to anyone who visits the Service, and search engines may index them.
  • Service providers. We use companies that help us run the Service, such as hosting, payment gateway, email delivery, analytics, customer support, and fraud-prevention providers [list or categorize as accurate]. They may use your information only to provide services to us, under contract.
  • Advertising and analytics partners. Where we use advertising or analytics cookies, the providers that set them may receive information about your use of the Service (see Section 5).
  • Legal reasons and protection. We may disclose information to comply with law, court orders, or lawful government requests, to enforce our Terms of Use, to investigate fraud or security issues, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Yacht Affair, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. If we merge with, are acquired by, or sell part or all of our business to another company, we may transfer your information as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent. We share information when you ask or allow us to.

We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you.

We do not sell your personal information for money.

We do not hold or move your money. Yacht Affair does not process, hold, or transfer payments between users, and we do not receive payment details you give to a broker, seller, or charter company.

05Cookies, Advertising, and Analytics

We and our service providers use cookies, pixels, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, measure how the Service is used, and, where we use them, deliver and measure advertising. They fall into three groups:

  • Essential: needed for the Service to work, such as login and security. These cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use the Service, so we can improve it.
  • Advertising: used to show and measure advertisements, including interest-based advertising across websites and devices.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or another place where the law requires it, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies. You can change your choices at any time using the cookie settings on the Service [or "Your Privacy Choices" link], and you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the Service works. Our Cookie Policy gives more detail about the cookies we use.

Opting out of interest-based ads. Besides our settings, you can opt out of many advertising networks through the Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (youradchoices.ca), the Network Advertising Initiative (optout.networkadvertising.org), or your device's advertising settings. Opting out of tailored ads does not stop you from seeing ads. They simply become less relevant.

06Fraud Prevention and Security

Fraud prevention. Yacht and boat sales are frequent targets for scams. To protect our users, we may review listings, accounts, and messages, and use automated tools [and third-party fraud-prevention services] to detect fraud, spam, bots, and other prohibited activity. These tools may look at device and browser characteristics, IP address, how you interact with the Service, and account and listing details. If our systems flag activity as high risk, we may restrict an action, hold a listing for review, or remove a listing or account. If you think we made a mistake, email info@yachtaffair.com and we will review it. We do not use this process to collect your date of birth, financial account numbers, or government ID numbers.

How we protect your information. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards for personal information, such as [encryption in transit, access controls, secure hosting, and regular backups]. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information sent to or stored on the Service or the internet.

If something goes wrong. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires. For example, under PIPEDA we report breaches that pose a real risk of significant harm, and under the EU and UK GDPR we report qualifying breaches to the regulator within 72 hours.

Your part. Use a strong, unique password, do not share your login details, and tell us right away at info@yachtaffair.com if you think your account has been accessed without permission. Be careful with emails or messages that claim to be from Yacht Affair or another user, and never send money or sensitive details to someone you have not verified. We will never ask you for your password.

07Third-Party Links and Websites

The Service contains advertisements, listings, and links that take you to websites and services run by other companies, including yacht and boat brokers, dealers, charter brokers and charter companies, surveyors, insurers, lenders, transport companies, and other service providers. When you click through, you leave the Service, and this policy no longer applies. Those companies set their own rules for collecting, using, and sharing your information, and they may use cookies and tracking tools we do not control.

We do not endorse these third parties and are not responsible for their privacy practices, security, or content. Please read their privacy policies before you give them any personal information, especially payment details, identity documents, or financial information. Section 11 of our Terms of Use explains our role further.

09Where Your Information Is Stored and International Transfers

We are based in Canada, and your information may be processed in Canada, the United States, and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live, and courts, law enforcement, and other authorities there may be able to access your information under their laws.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, we transfer your personal information outside those regions only with a lawful safeguard in place, such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses, or another mechanism the law recognizes. If you are in Canada, we use contracts and other measures to require service providers outside Canada to protect your information to a comparable standard. By using the Service, you understand that your information may be handled in these places.

11How to Contact Us and Make a Request

To use any of your rights, ask a privacy question, or raise a concern, email info@yachtaffair.com. (add a web form)

  • Verifying you: we may ask for information to confirm it is you before acting, so that we do not give your information to the wrong person.
  • Authorized agents: you may use an agent to make a request for you, and we may ask for proof of their authority.
  • Timing: we respond within the time the law requires, generally 30 days (45 days for some US requests), and we will tell you if we need more time.
  • Cost: requests are free, unless the law allows a fee for repeated or unreasonable requests.
  • Marketing: to stop marketing emails, use the unsubscribe link in any message or email us.

12Children and Changes to This Policy

Children. The Service is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email info@yachtaffair.com and we will delete it.

Changes. We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version with a new "as of" date at the top, and for material changes we will notify you by email or a notice on the Service before they take effect, as the law requires. Please check back for the latest version. Continuing to use the Service after a change means you accept the updated policy, except where the law requires your consent.
