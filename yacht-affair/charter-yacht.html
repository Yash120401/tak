Overview

Amara is a 40-metre Benetti Oasis offered for crewed charter, cruising the Arabian Gulf in winter and the Mediterranean in summer. Her fold-down beach club opens the stern to the water on three sides, making her a natural choice for swimming, water toys and long lunches at anchor.

Ten guests are accommodated in five cabins, including a full-beam main-deck owner’s suite, looked after by a crew of seven.

Rates, availability, deposits and cancellation terms are set by the charter company, who will confirm them with you before you book.
