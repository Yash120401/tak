Design direction

Concept B · Deep Water

A modern, ambitious yacht brand built on the two blues of the YA logo, set on deep water. Premium and professional, without the navy-and-gold look of traditional maritime sites.

Overall feel

How the client’s brief translates into the design.

Clean layouts with generous whitespace

100 px between sections on desktop, 64 px on mobile, on a 12-column grid.

Large, confident photography

A full-bleed hero video, then image-led cards and sections throughout.

Contemporary typography

Sora for headings and Inter for text, both free on Google Fonts.

Restrained premium feel

A dark base with one accent colour and quiet, glass-like panels.

A limited palette

Two blues from the logo, four base tones and two text tones.

Photography-led design

Dark gradients keep text readable over any photograph.

Colour palette

The palette is pulled from the logo: the A’s sky blue and the Y’s cobalt. Instead of classic navy, the base is a deep, near-black ink, so the blues read as light on water rather than as maritime livery. The one warm colour is reserved for the Under Offer status.

Imagery treatment

Real yacht photography leads every section; AI-generated photography is not used. Images sit under a dark gradient so text stays readable. The hero dims the video everywhere except inside the YA letterforms, the logo effect the client asked for. Fixed aspect ratios keep every upload cropped consistently.
