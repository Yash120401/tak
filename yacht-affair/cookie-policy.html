01What This Policy Covers

This Cookie Policy explains how Yacht Affair ("Yacht Affair," "we," "us"), based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, uses cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, scripts, local storage, software development kits, and similar technologies (together, "cookies") on yachtaffair.com, our related sites, and our mobile apps (the "Service"). We also authorize other companies to use cookies on the Service.

This policy is part of our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle the personal information that cookies collect. In this policy, "cookies" includes the technologies covered by the EU ePrivacy rules and similar laws, including Canada's PIPEDA and anti-spam rules, Quebec's privacy law, the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, and US state laws.

02What Cookies Are and Why We Use Them

A cookie is a small piece of data that a website asks your browser to store on your computer or mobile device. Cookies can make a website easier to use, for example by keeping you signed in and remembering your language and settings.

We use cookies to:

  • Keep the Service working and secure, including keeping you logged in and protecting accounts and listings from fraud.
  • Remember your choices, such as your language, currency, and saved searches.
  • Understand how visitors use the Service, so we can improve it.
  • Show and measure advertising, including advertising for the Service on other websites and apps, and advertising from our advertisers on the Service.
  • Let you share listings and sign in through social media features.

03The Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies. These are needed for the Service to work and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set when you do something such as log in, set your privacy choices, submit a form, or move between pages. We also use them for security, such as detecting fraud and violations of our policies. You can set your browser to block them, but parts of the Service will then not work.

Functionality cookies. These remember choices you make, such as your language or currency, and give you improved and personalized features. They may be set by us or by providers whose services we have added to our pages. If you do not allow them, some of these features may not work properly.

Performance and analytics cookies. These collect information about how visitors use the Service, such as the pages they view, how long they stay, and where they came from. We use this information to measure and improve the Service. We aim to use it in aggregated form.

Marketing and advertising cookies. These are used to show offers and advertisements that are more relevant to you, to limit how often you see an advertisement, and to measure how well advertising works. They are usually set by advertising networks and other partners, and they help those partners learn which people see and interact with advertisements, visit their websites, or use their apps. They may track you across different websites and over time.

Social media cookies. These support social media features, such as "Share" buttons and sign-in through a social media account. These features are hosted by the social media company or by us. Your use of them is governed by the privacy policy of the company that provides them.

04First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

First-party cookies are set by Yacht Affair when you visit or use the Service. Third-party cookies are set by other companies, such as our analytics providers, payment gateway, advertising partners, and social media platforms, when you use the Service. Either kind may last only for your visit (a "session" cookie) or stay on your device for repeat visits (a "persistent" cookie).

Do other parties use cookies on our Service? Yes. Other companies place cookies on the Service to help us measure usage and to help tailor advertisements to you on other websites and apps. We do not control those companies' cookies, and each company's own privacy policy governs what it does with the information it collects. The partners we use are listed in Section 5.

No control, no responsibility. Third-party cookies are set and controlled by those companies, not by us. We do not control what they collect, how long they keep it, who they share it with, or how they use it. To the fullest extent the law allows, Yacht Affair is not responsible or liable for third-party cookies, tags, or pixels, for those companies' practices or security, or for any loss, damage, or disclosure that results from them. Your dealings with each company are governed by its own terms and privacy policy. The limits of liability in our Terms of Use apply to this policy.

Lists may change. The companies and cookies we use can change at any time and without notice, and any list in this policy is given for information and may not be complete or current at every moment.

Your acceptance. By using the Service and accepting cookies, or by not refusing them where the law allows, you accept the risks of third-party cookies described here. If you do not accept them, refuse or block non-essential cookies, or do not use the Service.

06Your Rights and Choices

We use cookies that identify you, your computer, or your device only if you have agreed, or if we have a legitimate reason, such as making the Service work. You have the right at any time to:

  • Withdraw your agreement to the use of cookies.
  • Refuse cookies altogether, other than strictly necessary ones.
  • Delete cookies that have already been set.
  • Change your cookie settings.

Cookie banner. Before we set non-essential cookies, we show you a cookie banner. You can accept all, refuse all, or choose by category. In places where the law requires consent first, including the EEA, the UK, and Quebec, we set non-essential cookies only after you agree. Your choices apply to the Yacht Affair websites you visit on that browser. You can change them at any time using the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of any page [insert link].

Global Privacy Control. [Confirm your site supports this before keeping it.] If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising on that browser. We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" signals, because there is no common standard for them.

If you block or delete cookies, some parts of the Service may not work properly or as effectively.

Your responsibility for your choices. Your cookie choices apply only to the browser or device where you made them, and they may be lost if you clear your cookies or browsing data. We use reasonable efforts to honour your choices, but we cannot guarantee that every partner will honour them, or that a browser, extension, or device setting will not interfere with our banner or tools. To the fullest extent the law allows, we are not liable if a partner does not respect your choices.

Effect on the Service. If you refuse or block cookies, we may not be able to provide some features, such as login, saved settings, or personalized content, and we are not liable for any resulting loss or inconvenience. We may limit or refuse service where cookies are needed for security or to prevent fraud.

Records of your choices. We keep a record of your cookie choices and the time you made them, so that we can show we followed the law.

Continued use. In places where the law allows consent to be implied, continuing to use the Service after seeing our cookie banner means you accept the cookies described in this policy, until you change your settings.

Rights that cannot be waived. Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the law that cannot lawfully be waived, or excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

07Managing Cookies and Opting Out of Third-Party Ads

In your browser. Most browsers show what cookies are stored on your device, let you delete them one by one or all at once, and let you block cookies from all websites or only selected ones. You can usually turn off third-party cookies separately. Browser and device settings often apply only to that browser or device, so you may need to repeat them. Look in your browser's "Help" section for instructions.

Opting out of targeted advertising. To learn more about advertising cookies and how to opt out, you can visit:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada (Canada)
  • Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page and Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page (United States)
  • Your Online Choices (Europe)
  • Google Ads Settings, to customize or opt out of Google ads
  • The ad settings in your TikTok and Meta accounts, to control personalized ads from those platforms

If you opt out of tailored advertising, you may still see advertising, but it will be less relevant to you. You can find more general information about cookies at allaboutcookies.org.

08Other Websites, Changes, and Contact

Other websites. The Service contains links and advertisements that lead to other websites, including those of brokers, dealers, charter companies, and other service providers. This Cookie Policy applies only to the Service. When you click through to another website, read its own cookie and privacy policies, because we do not control its cookies.

Changes. We review this policy regularly and may update it, for example when we add or remove a cookie partner or when the law changes. We will post the new version with a new "as of" date at the top, and where the law requires it we will ask for your consent again.

Contact. For questions about this policy or your cookie choices, email info@yachtaffair.com. For information about how we handle personal information more broadly, see our Privacy Policy.
