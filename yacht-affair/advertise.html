Who can advertise

Built for the professionals and owners behind every listing

Yacht Affair brings brokers, dealers, private owners and charter operators together with buyers and charterers in a single marketplace for sale and charter, with transparent pricing and straightforward terms.

Brokers and dealers

Hold every licence and registration the law requires where you operate and where each yacht is located, with a written listing or sales agreement for each yacht you advertise.

Private sellers

Own the yacht or be authorised to sell it, free of liens and claims you have not disclosed.

Charter companies

Be lawfully allowed to offer each charter in the area where it operates, with the required certifications or inspections, insurance and properly licensed crew.

Every advertiser must be at least 18, able to form a binding contract, give accurate identity and contact details, and have the legal right to advertise each yacht or service listed. See the Advertiser Agreement.

Billing questions

Fees, renewals and refunds

What currency are prices in?

All prices are set in U.S. dollars (USD). At checkout you may choose to pay in USD, euros (EUR), or British pounds (GBP). If you choose EUR or GBP, the USD price is converted at the exchange rate shown at checkout. Your bank, card issuer, or payment provider may charge additional conversion fees.

How are payments processed?

Fees are due in advance and processed through our third-party payment gateway, Stripe, which offers multiple payment methods. We do not store your full card details.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Advertising subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period for the same period, at the then-current price, unless you cancel. We will remind you before renewal where the law requires it.

How do I cancel?

Email subscriptions@yachtaffair.com at least [30] days before the renewal date, preferably from the email address on your account. Cancelling stops future renewal charges, and your advertisements stay live until the end of the period you already paid for.

Can I get a refund?

To the extent the law allows, fees are non-refundable once your listing is published, even if you withdraw the listing early or do not complete a sale or charter. We may give a refund or credit at our discretion, or where the law or the Advertiser Agreement requires it.

Do fees include tax?

Fees do not include taxes unless we say so. You are responsible for all applicable taxes, such as GST, VAT, or sales tax, and we may add them to your invoice.

Where will my listing appear?

We decide where and in what order advertisements appear, and paying a fee does not guarantee any particular position, number of views, or results. We may review advertisements before or after they go live.

What can I do with enquiries?

You may use a lead only to respond to that enquiry and to carry out the deal the user asked about. You may not sell, rent or share a lead, or add the user to a marketing list without the consent the law requires.
