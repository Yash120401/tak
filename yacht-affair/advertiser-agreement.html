01Agreement and Acceptance

This Advertiser Agreement (the "Agreement") is between you and Bogati Continental Inc., doing business as Yacht Affair ("Yacht Affair," "we," "us"). It applies when you create an advertiser account, post a listing, or buy an advertising package on yachtaffair.com and our related sites and apps (the "Service"). "You" means the person or company advertising, and if you advertise for a company, you confirm you can bind it.

This Agreement works together with our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy, which also apply to you. If they conflict with this Agreement on a point about advertising, this Agreement controls. By checking "I agree," creating an account, posting a listing, or paying a fee, you accept all three documents. If you do not accept them, do not advertise on the Service.

The version of this Agreement that applies to you is the one posted on the Service when you place or renew an order. We may update it as described in the Terms of Use.

02Who May Advertise

You may advertise only if you:

  • Are at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract, and are not suspended or banned from the Service.
  • Give us accurate identity and contact details and keep them current.
  • Have the legal right to advertise each yacht or service you list.

If you are a broker or dealer, you also confirm that you hold every license and registration the law requires where you operate and where each yacht is located, that you have a written listing or sales agreement with the owner authorizing you to advertise it, and that you will disclose your role as the owner's agent.

If you are a private seller, you confirm that you own the yacht or are authorized to sell it, and that it is free of liens and claims you have not disclosed.

If you are a charter company or operator, you confirm that you are lawfully allowed to offer each charter in the area where it operates, and that you hold the required commercial vessel certifications or inspections (such as a Coast Guard certificate of inspection or the flag-state equivalent), insurance, and properly licensed crew.

On request, you will promptly give us proof of identity, ownership, authority, licensing, certification, or insurance. We may suspend or remove listings or accounts that cannot supply it. You may not transfer your account to anyone else without our written consent.

03Your Listings and Content

You are solely responsible for everything you post or send through the Service, including listing text, photos, videos, prices, availability, specifications, and messages (your "Content"). By posting it, you promise that:

  • It is true, accurate, complete, and not misleading, and you will update or remove it promptly when it changes. This includes marking a yacht as sold or under contract within [2] business days, and removing charter dates that are no longer available.
  • You have the right to use all text, photos, and other material in it, and it does not infringe anyone's rights.
  • Prices are stated clearly, and you disclose any taxes, fees, deposits, and other charges that apply, as well as whether a price is subject to change.
  • It identifies you, and the contact method you give lets users reach you directly. You will not charge users for information about a listing.
  • You will place it in the right category, with photos that show the actual yacht or service offered.
  • It does not discriminate, or intend to discriminate, on any ground the law protects, such as race, colour, religion, sex, national origin, disability, or family status.

When you offer a yacht for sale or charter, you must be ready to sell or provide it on the terms you advertised. Statements that are inaccurate or misleading harm user trust in you and in Yacht Affair, and we may remove such Content and the account behind it.

04Prohibited Conduct

While advertising on the Service, you will not:

  • Post Content that is false, misleading, defamatory, obscene, or unlawful, or that infringes someone's rights, or post a yacht or service you are not authorized to offer.
  • Take payment for, or fail to deliver, a yacht or service in a way that is fraudulent, or advertise a yacht or charter you do not intend to provide ("bait and switch").
  • Manipulate prices, interfere with other advertisers' listings, or post duplicate or fake listings to gain placement.
  • Avoid or manipulate our fees, billing process, or listing packages, including by sharing an account or listing more yachts than your package allows.
  • Use the Service to promote another website, product, or service without our prior written permission, except as the listing format allows.
  • Send spam, chain letters, or unsolicited bulk messages, or collect or use other users' information, including email addresses, without their consent.
  • Distribute viruses or other harmful code, or interfere with or overload the Service.
  • Use any robot, spider, scraper, or other automated or manual systematic method to copy, monitor, or collect data from the Service, or bypass our access controls, without our prior written permission.
  • Copy, modify, or distribute Yacht Affair content, trademarks, or other material without our permission.
  • Break any law or any rule in our Terms of Use.

05Our Role: An Advertising Platform Only

Yacht Affair is an advertising venue. We bring advertisers and interested users together, and we are not involved in any sale, charter, or other deal between them. In particular:

  • We do not own, inspect, survey, or verify any yacht, listing, or user, and we do not guarantee the quality, safety, legality, condition, or availability of anything advertised, or the accuracy of any listing.
  • We are not a broker, agent, charter operator, escrow agent, or party to any transaction.
  • We do not process, hold, or transfer money between users. Purchase prices, deposits, charter fees, commissions, and other amounts are arranged and paid directly between you and the other party, outside the Service. We do not recommend or endorse any payment method or escrow provider.
  • We do not transfer title or ownership, handle registration or documentation, or arrange delivery. Those are matters between you and the other party.
  • We cannot verify that a user is who they say they are or that a payment you receive is valid. You should use sound judgment and carry out your own checks before you accept or send any payment.

Prices on the Service often leave out taxes, finance charges, registration or compliance fees, and other costs. The price and all other terms of any deal are for you and the other party to negotiate directly.

If you choose EUR or GBP, the USD price is converted at the exchange rate shown at checkout. Your bank, card issuer, or payment provider may charge additional conversion fees.

Payment. Fees are due in advance. They are processed through our third-party payment gateway, STRIPE, LLC, which offers multiple payment methods. We do not store your full card details. You must use a valid payment method and keep it current. If a payment fails or your account is past due, we may suspend or remove your listings, and we may collect the amount owed by other lawful means, including charging another payment method you have on file [with your authorization], using a collection agency, or taking legal action. You are responsible for the costs of collection to the extent the law allows.

Taxes. Fees do not include taxes unless we say so. You are responsible for all applicable taxes, such as GST, VAT, or sales tax, and we may add them to your invoice.

Automatic renewal. Advertising subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period for the same period, at the then-current price, unless you cancel. We will remind you before renewal where the law requires it.

How to cancel. To cancel an advertising subscription, email subscriptions@yachtaffair.com at least [30] days before the renewal date, preferably from the email address on your account. Cancelling stops future renewal charges. Your advertisements stay live until the end of the period you already paid for.

No refunds. To the extent the law allows, fees are non-refundable once your listing is published, even if you gave wrong information, withdrew the listing early, or did not complete a sale or charter. We may give a refund or credit at our discretion, or where the law or this Agreement requires it, such as when we cannot display your advertisement for a long period (see the Force Majeure clause in our Terms of Use).

Changes to fees. We may change our fees, packages, rates, and specifications for future periods on at least [30] days' notice. If you do not agree, cancel before the change takes effect.

Our liability for an error in your advertisement is limited as set out in Section 12. We accept no liability for errors or omissions in advertisements, and you should check your advertisement as soon as it is published and tell us at once of any mistake.

You also promise to follow all economic sanctions and export control laws of Canada, the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom, and any other place that applies to you ("Sanctions Laws"). You will not advertise, sell, charter, or deal in a yacht or service that is located in, or connected with, a country or region that is subject to comprehensive sanctions under those laws, or with any person or entity that is on a sanctions or restricted-party list, or that is 50 percent or more owned or controlled by such a person, where the deal would break a Sanctions Law. You confirm that none of the following is on any such list: you, your owners, directors, and beneficial owners, and any yacht you advertise and its owners.

Your continuing promises. Every time you post, renew, or update a listing, you confirm that:

  • Neither you, nor any of your owners, directors, officers, or beneficial owners, nor any yacht or service you advertise, nor its owner, operator, flag state, or crew, is the target of Sanctions Laws, and none is located, ordinarily resident, or organized in a country or region that is the subject of comprehensive sanctions.
  • No yacht you advertise is blocked or frozen property, or has been used to avoid or evade sanctions, for example by hiding its identity, ownership, or location, switching off or tampering with its tracking transponder, changing its flag to conceal ownership, or making unreported transfers at sea.
  • No money, property, or service connected with your advertising, your listings, or the fees you pay us comes from, or is for the benefit of, a sanctioned person or place.
  • Your listings and your dealings do not help anyone else break Sanctions Laws.

Your duty to screen. You alone are responsible for screening every buyer, charterer, owner, yacht, and other party you deal with against all sanctions and restricted-party lists that apply, and for deciding whether a deal is lawful. We do not screen your dealings, and you may not rely on us, on our review of a listing, or on any verification badge as proof that a yacht, person, or deal complies with Sanctions Laws.

Tell us at once. You must notify us immediately at info@yachtaffair.com if you or any yacht you advertise becomes, or you think may become, subject to Sanctions Laws, or if you receive a sanctions-related inquiry or order about your listings. On request, you must promptly give us the information and documents we reasonably ask for to check compliance, such as ownership details, registration, and proof of identity. A failure to do so is a breach of this Agreement.

Our rights. We may remove any listing, suspend or end any account, and refuse or stop any service that we believe breaks this section or may expose us to a penalty or legal risk, at any time, with or without notice and without refund. We may also report suspected breaches to law enforcement and regulators, freeze or withhold amounts where the law requires it, and cooperate with any investigation.

Indemnity for sanctions breaches. You will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Yacht Affair and its owners, affiliates, directors, officers, employees, agents, and licensors from all fines, penalties, losses, damages, costs, and expenses, including reasonable legal and investigation fees, that arise out of or relate to your breach of this section or any actual or alleged breach of Sanctions Laws connected with your listings or dealings. We are not liable to you or anyone else for any loss, delay, or cost caused by our removing a listing, suspending an account, refusing service, freezing amounts, or making a report under this section, or by our compliance with any law, order, or request of an authority, even if it later turns out that you did nothing wrong. No contrary obligation. Nothing in this Agreement requires either party to act in a way that would break a law that applies to it, including any law that blocks compliance with another country's sanctions. If this section and any other part of this Agreement conflict, this section controls.

You are responsible for handling leads and other personal information you receive in line with the privacy laws that apply to you, such as PIPEDA and Alberta's PIPA, and, where they apply, the EU and UK GDPR and US state laws. You must keep that information secure, delete it when you no longer need it, and tell us promptly at info@yachtaffair.com if you suspect a breach involving information you received through the Service.

We handle personal information as described in our Privacy Policy. We do not sell or rent your personal information to others for their own marketing without your consent. If you object to the transfers or uses described there, please do not advertise on the Service.
