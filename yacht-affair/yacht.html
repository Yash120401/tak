Overview

Azure Meridian is a 40-metre Benetti Oasis delivered in 2020 and based in Dubai. Her signature fold-down beach club opens the stern to the water on three sides, creating one of the most generous outdoor spaces in her class.

Ten guests are accommodated in five cabins, including a full-beam main-deck owner’s suite. A crew of seven is accommodated forward, with separate guest and crew circulation throughout.

She has had one owner since delivery and is offered with her maintenance history. Viewings can be arranged through the listing broker.
