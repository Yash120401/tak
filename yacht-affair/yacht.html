Description

About this yacht

The Great Northern Lights of Vancouver is a 46-metre Feadship delivered in 2018 and based on Canada’s west coast. Built for long passages in comfort, she pairs a steel displacement hull with a range of 4,500 nautical miles at twelve knots.

Twelve guests are accommodated in six cabins, including a full-beam main-deck master suite with a private study. Generous outdoor decks, a sundeck spa pool and a large tender garage make her equally suited to coastal cruising and extended voyages.

She has been maintained by a full-time crew of ten and is offered with her complete service history. She is currently under offer; the listing brokerage will register back-up interest.
