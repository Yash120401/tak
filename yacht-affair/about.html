About Yacht Affair

Connecting the yachting world.

Yacht Affair is an online marketplace for yachts and boats for sale and for charter. The platform brings together brokers, dealers, private owners and charter operators with buyers, sellers and charterers in a single, easy-to-use destination.

Everyone with a stake in the yacht market

Yacht Affair serves everyone with a stake in the yacht market, from professional brokers, dealers and charter operators to private owners, and from first-time charterers to experienced buyers.

Whatever the size of the vessel or the scale of the business, advertisers gain a direct line to interested buyers and guests, and anyone searching has a single place to find, compare and contact sellers and operators.

How we operate

An advertising platform for the yachting industry

Yacht Affair is an advertising platform that gives yachting industry professionals, from brokers and dealers to charter operators and marine service providers, a dedicated space to present their yachts, boats and services to a broad audience of interested parties.

  1. 01

    Advertisers present their yachts, boats and services

    Brokers, dealers, private owners, charter operators and marine service providers list on one platform.

  2. 02

    Visitors browse and enquire

    Visitors browse listings and make enquiries as they choose.

  3. 03

    The parties deal directly

    Any resulting transaction is conducted directly between the parties involved.

A trustworthy marketplace

We are committed to maintaining a trustworthy marketplace. Advertisers are required to describe their yachts accurately, and reports of misleading or fraudulent listings are reviewed and acted upon where appropriate.

Our story

Yacht Affair was founded on the belief that advertising and finding a yacht or boat online should be clear, fair and efficient.

What the industry told us

  • Brokers have long raised concerns about rising listing fees.
  • Owners and charter operators often must spread their inventory across multiple sites.
  • Buyers must remain alert to fraud.

How Yacht Affair answers

  • A single marketplace for sale and charter
  • Transparent pricing
  • Straightforward terms

As the industry evolves, Yacht Affair will continue to develop the platform that its advertisers and visitors rely on.
